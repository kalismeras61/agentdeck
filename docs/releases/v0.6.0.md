**Bots that do more than code, and reach you on your phone.**

**Telegram.** Connect a Telegram bot of your own (Settings → Telegram: make one with @BotFather and paste its token, which stays in your keychain) and pair your chat with a one-time code.
- Give bots work from your phone: write a task and the bot you picked gets it; its answer comes back as a reply. Start with a bot's name ("Reviewer: look at PR 12") to pick another. `/bots`, `/status`, `/cancel`.
- Answer what they wait on: a risky step arrives with Allow / Deny, a plan with Approve plan / Keep planning, a question with its choices (or reply with your own words).
- Results of scheduled tasks and triggers come there too (or every task, or none: your choice).
- AgentDeck asks Telegram for new messages itself: nothing on your Mac is opened to the internet, and only private chats you paired are answered.

**Connections.** Settings → Connections: a browser (Playwright), GitHub, Notion, your Mac's Calendar, Reminders, Mail and Notes, or any MCP server by command or address, each with your own account. Pick per bot what it may use. What a connection reads is allowed while a bot works; what it changes waits for you (in AgentDeck or on Telegram) unless you let it act on its own. Bots open mail drafts; they never send mail.

**Triggers.** A bot's new Triggers tab: a web page changes, new files land in a folder, an issue or pull request gets the `agentdeck` label, or a script on your Mac calls the bot's hook. Bots can also tell you something right away (`notify_me`).

**Ready-made bots.** Start a bot from a Researcher, Inbox assistant, Day planner, Social media writer, Watcher or Developer.

**Download `AgentDeck-0.6.0.dmg`**: one app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later, signed and notarized. Installed copies update themselves.
