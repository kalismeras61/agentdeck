**Your bots on Telegram.** Connect a Telegram bot of your own (Settings → Telegram: make one with @BotFather and paste its token, which stays in your keychain) and pair your chat with a one-time code. Then:

- **Give bots work from your phone.** Write a task and the bot you picked gets it; its answer comes back as a reply. Start with a bot's name ("Reviewer: look at PR 12") to pick another. `/bots`, `/status`, `/cancel`.
- **Answer what they wait on.** A risky step arrives with Allow / Deny, a plan with Approve plan / Keep planning, a question with its choices (or reply with your own words). Answer it in AgentDeck instead and the Telegram message says so.
- **Hear from scheduled tasks.** Results of scheduled tasks come to Telegram too; choose nothing else, or every bot task, in Settings.

AgentDeck asks Telegram for new messages itself: nothing on your Mac is opened to the internet, there's no server in between, and only private chats you paired are answered.

**Download `AgentDeck-0.6.0.dmg`**: one app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later, signed and notarized. Installed copies update themselves.
