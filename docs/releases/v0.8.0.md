**Work as a team.** A new Team view (⌘⇧U) shows who works on which branch of a project, what their agents are doing, and where your work and theirs meet. It runs on the git remote you already push to: no account, no server.

- **Branches and people.** Every branch on the remote with who committed to it, how far it is from `main` and which files it changes. Branches that change the same files are flagged before anyone merges. Turn on *Share my work* and teammates see your branch and your agents (working, waiting for you, idle) live; you see exactly what's shared before it's turned on.
- **Review a teammate's branch.** Read its commits and diffs without switching, check it out in its own folder next to the project (your working copy isn't touched), update it when they push again, or let an agent review it with one click.
- **Activity and notifications.** New branches, pushes, merges, `main` moving ahead of your branches, and a teammate's branch starting to touch your files, as a feed and, for what matters, as notifications.
- **Messages, and agents that talk to each other.** Ask a teammate a question, write to everyone, or ask for a review. Your agents can do the same (`ask_teammate`, and wait for the answer), and a teammate can let their agent answer from their code. Every message that leaves your Mac waits for your approval.
- **Time.** How long your agents worked, how long they waited for you, and how long you spent with them, per day, branch and session, with a CSV timesheet. Teammates who share see each other's time per branch.
- **AgentDeck Relay.** For instant delivery without push access, run the new self-hosted relay (one binary or a Docker image), create a team, and share the invite. See `relay/README.md`.
