**Bots.** A bot is an agent that stays: give it a name, a job, instructions, an agent and model (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok Build, Kimi Code, Antigravity or your own models), and a folder, then hand it tasks. It works through them one at a time in the background while you do something else.

- **A task queue.** Add tasks while the bot is busy; pause, resume, cancel or retry them. Each finished task keeps the bot's report as its result.
- **Memory.** Every bot keeps a short memory file in its folder (`.agentdeck/bots/<name>.md`, left out of git). It reads it at the start of each task and writes down what's worth remembering. You can read and edit it on the bot's page.
- **Works unattended, safely.** While a bot works on a task, ordinary requests are approved for it; anything risky (deleting, pushing, deploying, credentials, anything outside its folder) and every question waits for you, and the bot shows "Needs you" in the sidebar.
- **Its own page.** The bot's conversation with diffs and approvals as in any session, a box to give it tasks (or send a plain message), and its queue and memory side by side.

Find Bots at the top of the sidebar, or start one from a project with the bot button. Schedules, bots that hand work to other bots, and reaching your bots from your phone or Telegram come next.

**Download `AgentDeck-0.4.0.dmg`**: one app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later, signed and notarized. Installed copies update themselves.
